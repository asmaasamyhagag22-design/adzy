# Independent review instructions

A coordinator should provide newly collected Arabic and English publisher
pages, fixed objectives and randomized package IDs to two reviewers. Exclude
the public development cases in this directory and the prior Campaign corpus.
Keep the key linking package IDs to candidate or baseline private. Do not
reveal predicted outcomes or discussion notes. Capture exact page bytes,
retrieval timestamp and hashes before running either release.

For each package, independently record:

- Subject and objective fit, including exact product or variant.
- Every consumer claim, its evidence source and exact supporting span.
- Conditions missing from the poster, risks from translation and an overall
  supported/uncertain/unsupported verdict.
- Actionability of the destination, usefulness of the editor's brief,
  Arabic/English readability at phone size and visual product fidelity.
- Whether blocking was proportionate and the smallest change that might safely
  restore a useful result.

Record every attempted case, including fetch failures and abstentions. Two
reviewers should label first, then adjudicate disagreement with the source
available. Report support precision, unsupported-copy acceptance, acceptance of
supported edits, abstention and useful task completion with denominators by
category and language. This is a blank independent-review instrument. The
current result files contain development predictions and should stay outside
the blinded review packet.
